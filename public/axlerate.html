    Home/Help centre

    Help centre

    Answers on pricing, fitment, shipping and returns. Can't find what you need? Call 1-800-555-0148 or send us a message.

    How does Axlerate set its prices?

    For each part we look up the current price at major US retailers, then take 20% off. The retail price we compared against is crossed out on every listing, so you can see the saving. Prices were last checked in October 2026 and can change.

    How do I know a part fits my vehicle?

    Use the vehicle finder on the home page or the Shop page. Each listing shows the vehicles it is listed for. Size-based parts such as tires, batteries, bulbs and wipers are marked for you to check against the size on your current part or in your owner's manual.

    How fast do orders ship?

    Orders placed before 3 pm ET on a weekday ship the same business day. Orders placed later, or on a weekend, ship the next business day. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days and express takes 1 to 2 business days.

    What does shipping cost?

    Standard shipping is free on orders of $75 or more and $7.99 on smaller orders. Express shipping is $14.99. We ship to addresses in the United States.

    Do you install parts or mount tires?

    No. We ship parts only. Tires are sent unmounted, so you will need a shop to mount and balance them.

    Are the parts new?

    Yes. Every part is new and sold under the brand named on its listing.

    Do parts come with a warranty?

    The manufacturer's warranty applies and the length depends on the product. For example, the Optima RedTop battery has a 36-month warranty, Sylvania bulbs have 12 months, and the Michelin Defender2 has an 80,000-mile treadwear warranty. Check each product's packaging for the full terms.

    How do I track my order?

    Open Track order and enter your order number and email.

    Returns and refunds

    You can return unused parts in their original packaging within 30 days of delivery. We refund the original payment method once the part arrives back at our warehouse.

    • Wrong part, faulty part or damaged in transit: the return label is free.
    • Changed your mind: a $7.99 label fee is taken from your refund.

    Fit guarantee

    If a part we listed for your vehicle does not fit, we pay for the return and send the correct part. This covers parts listed for a specific year, make and model. For size-based parts, please check the size on your current part or in your owner's manual before ordering.
